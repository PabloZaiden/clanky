Clanky Privacy Policy

Clanky connects to your own self-hosted service. Messages, files and audio are processed by your service and its configured providers to perform your requests, under their privacy policies.

Clanky does not send telemetry to the developer. Hosting providers receive IP addresses and request information to deliver content.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and message.

Retention and deletion

Your service and providers determine retention and deletion of the data they hold. Contact their operators to request deletion. Disconnect to stop sharing new content.

Support emails are kept only to respond to your request. Request deletion using the contact below.
