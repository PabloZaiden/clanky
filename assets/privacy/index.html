Clanky Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026.

Clanky is published by Pablo Zaidenvoren. It is client software for connecting to an existing, independently operated Clanky controller. The publisher does not provide a hosted controller, a central Clanky account, or an AI service.

Information on your device

The client stores connection details, authentication sessions, preferences, drafts, and cached application data needed for the features you use. Browser clients and the Apple app use persistent web storage for sessions and application state. This is not a separate native Keychain credential store. Device and browser backup settings may affect copies of local data.

The Apple app also keeps limited local diagnostic events about application startup, connection restoration, and web storage. These diagnostics are not automatically sent to the publisher. They do not contain your messages, attachment contents, or authentication tokens.

Your controller and providers

You choose the controller or accept a collaboration invitation from its operator. Connected features transmit the information needed for your actions to that controller and its authorized execution hosts. This can include messages, selected files and images, instructions, recorded audio, and connection and authentication information.

Agents and commands run on the connected hosts, not on your iPhone or iPad. The controller can store your content and send it to the coding agents, AI and voice providers, collaboration relays, notification destinations, and other integrations configured by its operator. Their processing, access, retention, and any model-training practices depend on their configuration, terms, and privacy policies. Ask the controller operator about these destinations before sending confidential or personal data.

Connecting to a controller does not give the Clanky publisher access to its accounts, credentials, repositories, messages, or files. The publisher does not collect this content through a central Clanky service.

Device permissions

The microphone is used when you choose voice features; recorded audio can be processed by the controller and its configured voice provider. Camera and file access are used for attachments you choose. Local-network access supports connections to controllers on your network. You can revoke device permissions in system settings. Declining a permission does not authorize another way to access that protected resource.

Public infrastructure and support

Public project pages and distribution files use third-party hosting. Those hosts receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address and request timestamps, under their own privacy policies, including GitHub's privacy statement. These requests do not include your controller credentials or workspace content. Apple handles app distribution and any platform diagnostics under its own policies and your device settings.

If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send, including your email address and message. Do not include passwords, tokens, private source code, or other sensitive material. Support correspondence is used to respond to the request and retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations.

Retention, removal, and your choices

Local information can remain until you clear the relevant browser or app storage. Signing out revokes the applicable device session when the controller is reachable; it does not necessarily erase all cached data. Removing a local connection or uninstalling the app does not delete remote accounts, conversations, repositories, files, or backups, and does not stop work already running on a controller.

For remote account access, export, correction, deletion, or retention questions, contact the operator of your controller and the providers involved. The publisher cannot delete data from independently operated controllers. You can stop sending new information by disconnecting or ceasing use of the connected features. Contact us for privacy requests concerning information you provided directly to the publisher.

Local demonstration

The reserved server address https://demo-controller selects a simulation inside the client. It uses fictional data and simulated replies, does not connect to a controller or AI provider, and keeps demo changes only in memory until you leave or reload. Do not enter real secrets or personal data into the demonstration.

Changes to this policy

We will revise this page and its effective date when these practices change. Applicable law and any additional notice or permission requirements continue to apply.
